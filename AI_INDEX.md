# AI index: Rational Fibonacci–Catalan polynomials (v0.1.0-candidate)

Machine-oriented map of claims, evidence and limits. Human readers should start with [README.md](README.md) ("What
this is about") and the paper [manuscript.pdf](manuscript.pdf) (source [manuscript.tex](manuscript.tex); Section 1 is
a plain introduction: the objects, why they matter, what was known, what is new, and a roadmap).
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24541015 (concept 10.5281/zenodo.24541014). Status: unrefereed candidate; internal AI-assisted
review and exact computation only.

## Normalisation

[u]_q = 1 + q + ... + q^(u-1). F_0 = 0, F_1 = 1, F_{k+2} = F_{k+1} + F_k. P_{m,n}(q) = prod_{j=1..n} [F_{m+j}]_q / [F_j]_q
(q-Fibonomial coefficient); C_{m,n}(q) = P_{m,n}(q) / [F_{m+n}]_q (rational q-Fibonacci–Catalan function of
Bergeron-Ceballos-Kuestner, SIGMA 16 (2020) 076). g = gcd(m,n). Coxeter quotient: H_{D,a}(q) = prod_{d in D}
[F_{a+d-1}]_q / [F_d]_q for the degree multiset D of an irreducible finite Coxeter group, untwisted (unpermuted
exponents). Unimodal = coefficients nondecreasing up to the centre (P_{m,n} is symmetric). Width = min(m,n).

Every check uses exact arithmetic (integers and rationals). A few reported decimals (the density product in
claims.json; margins and error bounds in width*.json) are double-precision renderings made by single IEEE 754
operations or Fraction-to-float conversions, which are correctly rounded and so platform independent. Every check
raises an exception on failure, also under python -O.

Numbers quoted on the release page and in its audio: 15 (computed range of BCK), gcd <= 2, 2n - 3, about half, about
0.408, widths up to seven (previously three), the C_{4,3} coefficients 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 2, 3, 2, 2, 1, 1, the example
89/72/77/231/48, and B_3 with shift 3 giving [21]_q. All are proved in the paper and reproduced by the programs.

## Limits

The complete intersection gives a graded algebra and a monomial basis, NOT a combinatorial interpretation on the
path-domino tilings of BCK: their Open Problems 4.3 and 5.4 remain open in the intended sense (Remark 3.4, Open
Problem 1). The unrestricted unimodality conjecture (BCK Conj. 2.5) remains open. Widths five to seven and the
exceptional Coxeter rows rest on exact computer certificates. Galois-twisted non-crystallographic quotients at shifts
other than +-1 mod h, and reducible groups, are not treated (Open Problem 3). m_0(n) is not effective in general.
